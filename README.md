This calculator takes melee, ranged, and magic weapons into account and finds out their name, damage, power attack (critical hit), and the cost of the weapon. Each weapon type has a different critical hit rate that is calculated based on their typing, and cost value based on what type of weapon it is. 
For instance, melee weapons have a flat x2 to their damage if they get a critical hit, and only cost about 160% the damage that they do. For this reason, a melee weapon would be an even trade of money versus cost. Ranged weapons instead cost and deal less than melee, but the damage potential from a critical hit would do far more damage compared to those two factors. On the other end of the spectrum, magic weapons deal an already high amount of damage to begin with. In exchange for that, they cost more and have a weaker critical damage.
This would leave some speculation about the best weapon in general. For example, you might wonder why you'd ever buy a wand for 60 when an axe for 50 does more basic damage and has a higher crit. The simple answer is magic baby! You can't cast magic with an axe, but you can with a wand.
